Who we are

Welcome to Netball Munich!

We’re a group of expats in Munich who all love netball. We meet every week, enjoy a good game and are always keen to meet new members.

What we offer

Training. Socials. Tournaments.

Come along, meet the team and join our weekly training sessions and monthly socials. Players must be 18 or older and already understand the rules of netball. We can’t cater to absolute beginners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you taking on new members?

Yes! We welcome people with pre-existing knowledge of netball rules. Let us know via Instagram if you’re coming along to a session.

How do I become a member?

Come along for a free session and try out our training. If you’d like to join, fill in the registration form and pay your fees.

When and where is training held?

We train every Wednesday from 20:30–21:30 at Sophie-Scholl-Gymnasium in Schwabing (Gym Location). Head through the green gate and the sports hall is on the left. Please note that there is no training during school holidays, as the hall is closed.

How can I join the WhatsApp group?

After you come along to a training session, you can scan a QR code to join our WhatsApp Community for socials, fitness sessions and more. Once you’ve filled out the training form and paid your fees, you’ll be added to the Training WhatsApp group.

What are the membership fees?

After your free trial, if you’d like to join the club, we ask for fees to cover the sports hall, equipment and more. Fees are €30 for 6 months or €60 for 12 months.

What can I expect in training sessions?

Expect fun drills, game time and a great team atmosphere. Bring your court shoes, comfortable sportswear and a water bottle.

How do tournaments work?

Every year we’re invited to play in tournaments around Europe, such as Amsterdam, Zurich and Copenhagen. Anyone who attends training regularly and would like to join is welcome! We all book the same accommodation and try to travel over together, it’s always a great weekend.

Do I have to be a pro netballer?

No! We have people with a range of abilities and netball experience. However, we do ask that you’ve played netball before and know the rules.
